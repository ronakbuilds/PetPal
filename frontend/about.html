About PetPal

A kinder world for every animal

PetPal is an AI-powered platform that helps pet owners and animal lovers manage pet records, vaccination schedules, adoption services, and receive multilingual assistance through an intelligent AI assistant.

Mission

Empower every caregiver with intelligent tools

We want pet parents and stray-animal caregivers everywhere to have access to reliable, multilingual, AI-assisted care — regardless of location, income or experience.

Objectives

What PetPal sets out to do

  • ✓ Digitize pet health records
  • ✓ Vaccination reminders
  • ✓ Pet adoption & stray care
  • ✓ Multilingual AI Assistant

AI in Pet Care

Why intelligence matters

PetPal AI is a multilingual virtual assistant that helps users understand the website, manage pet records, answer pet-care questions, and provide vaccination reminders in their preferred language.

It's a website guide, a pet-care companion and a personalized reminder engine, all in one.
